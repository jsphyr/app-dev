# **Suits** {#suits-series}
 
## **Overview**
**Suits** is an American legal drama series created by *Aaron Korsh*.  
It tells the story of **Mike Ross**, a brilliant college dropout who starts practicing law at **Pearson Hardman** under the mentorship of **Harvey Specter**, despite never attending law school.

## **Chronological Overview (by Season)** {#suits-order}
1. **Season 1 (2011)** – Mike joins the firm and begins his secret double life as a lawyer.  
2. **Season 2 (2012)** – The firm faces internal power struggles; Harvey and Jessica battle for control.  
3. **Season 3 (2013)** – The firm merges with Darby International; tensions rise between Harvey and Mike.  
4. **Season 4 (2014)** – Mike leaves the firm to work as an investment banker, causing friction with Harvey.  
5. **Season 5 (2015)** – Mike’s secret is exposed; he faces legal consequences for practicing without a license.  
6. **Season 6 (2016)** – Mike serves prison time and later returns to the firm legitimately.  
7. **Season 7 (2017)** – Mike and Rachel prepare to leave the firm; Harvey becomes managing partner.  
8. **Season 8 (2018)** – New partners like Samantha Wheeler join; the firm rebrands as *Zane Specter Litt Wheeler Williams*.  
9. **Season 9 (2019)** – The series concludes with major character growth and emotional goodbyes.
